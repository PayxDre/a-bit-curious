Locks

Every coin is locked by a script. See how keys, multisig, and timelocks decide who can spend what.

Open the interactive lesson →

Every UTXO is protected by a locking script . Think of every UTXO as a locked treasure chest. To spend it, you must provide a valid cryptographic signature .

The lock is written in a simple programming language called Script . The most common lock says: "provide a public key that hashes to this address AND a valid signature." The matching unlock is the scriptSig .

Bitcoin allows complex locks too. Multisig requires multiple signatures. Time-locked UTXOs can't be spent until a future date.

🔒 Types of Bitcoin Locks

This lock-and-key system is Bitcoin's security foundation. Every full node independently verifies that every spending attempt satisfies the lock. Mathematics doesn't care who you are.

Every coin is locked by a small program that states the condition for spending it. Usually that is one signature. It can also be two of three keys, or a date in the future. Every node checks the condition.
