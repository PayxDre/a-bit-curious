Wallets

Your front door to Bitcoin · ~3 min read

A wallet holds keys, not coins. Hot vs cold, custodial vs self-custody, and how to choose your first one. This is the reading version - the interactive lesson has the live demos, quizzes, and progress tracking.

"Not your keys, not your coins." This is the most important phrase in all of Bitcoin. We'll explain exactly what it means right here.

Think of a Bitcoin wallet like a special email account - but instead of storing emails, it stores the secret codes that prove you own your Bitcoin. The wallet itself doesn't actually hold any coins (Bitcoin lives on the blockchain, a shared public record everyone can see). What the wallet holds is something far more important: your private key, which is like the master key to your safety deposit box at the bank.

There are several types of wallets. A hardware wallet is like a super-secure USB stick that keeps your keys offline and safe from hackers. A software wallet is an app on your phone - great for small amounts and everyday spending. A "paper wallet" is literally your key written on a piece of paper - old school, but completely hack-proof as long as you don't lose it or spill coffee on it!

The phrase "not your keys, not your coins" means this: if someone else (like an exchange) is holding your Bitcoin for you, they actually control it, not you. It's like letting a stranger hold your wallet and trusting they won't run away. When you control your own wallet and private key, you truly own your Bitcoin - no bank, no government, no company can freeze or seize it. That's the whole superpower of Bitcoin.

Key terms

secret codes
Called "private keys" - these are like the password to your Bitcoin vault. Whoever has the key controls the coins.
hardware wallet
A physical USB-like device (e.g., Ledger, Trezor) that keeps your private keys completely offline - hackers can't reach it over the internet.
software wallet
An app on your phone or computer (e.g., BlueWallet, Exodus). Convenient but slightly riskier than hardware wallets since it's connected to the internet.
