The Lab: Use Real Bitcoin

Use real Bitcoin on the Signet test network: generate keys, receive coins, sign and broadcast a genuine transaction.

Open the interactive lesson →

After this lesson you can explainhow to make a real Bitcoin transaction with your own hands.

Everything you have learned so far, you are now going to do. In the next ten minutes you will generate a real Bitcoin key pair, receive real coins, sign a real transaction with real ECDSA cryptography running in your browser, broadcast it to a live network, and watch a real miner seal it into a block that anyone on Earth can verify.

The network is Signet, Bitcoin's official test network. It runs the same software, the same cryptography, and the same rules as Bitcoin - but its coins are free and worthless by design. That makes it the perfect gym: real weights, zero risk.

⚠️ The two rules of the lab

1. Test coins only. The wallet you create here is for Signet. Never send real bitcoin to it.

2. This page generates keys inside your browser and never transmits them - there is no server. But the habit matters more than the fact: never type a real seed phrase or private key into any website. Ever. Including this one.

One click runs the full derivation you learned in Chapter VI: 256 random bits, secp256k1 point multiplication, SHA-256, RIPEMD-160, bech32. All of it in your browser, all of it real.

Copy your address above and paste it into a faucet - a site that gives away free Signet coins. This is a real payment from a stranger's wallet to yours.

This spends your coins back to your own address (minus a real miner fee), exactly like the coin picker demo taught - except this time the signature is real ECDSA, the sighash is real BIP 143, and the network will actually verify it.

Signet blocks arrive roughly every 10 minutes, just like Bitcoin. When a miner includes your transaction, it becomes permanent history.

Your transaction is now public data on a real blockchain. Pull it back down and take it apart: every byte you broadcast, what each one means, and - once it confirms - the block header that sealed it into history.

🎓 What you just did

You held a private key, received a UTXO, built a transaction that consumed it and created a new one, signed it with elliptic curve cryptography, paid a miner a fee, and got your payment sealed into a block by proof of work. That is the entire Bitcoin system, end to end, operated by you. Moving real bitcoin works exactly the same way - the only differences are the network name and the stakes.

Your transaction obeyed rules no company wrote and no government passed. Time to see how a leaderless network agrees on anything - and how it upgrades without breaking.

You created a key, received coins, built and signed a transaction and watched a block confirm it on a live test network. Real bitcoin works in exactly the same way. Only the stakes differ.
