Bitcoin's identity system has three main concepts: the private key , the public key , and the address . They work in a one-way chain: private key → public key → address.

The important property is that these derivations are one-way. You can never figure out someone's private key from their public key or address. This is the magic of elliptic curve cryptography .

Modern addresses start with "bc1q" (SegWit ) or "bc1p" (Taproot). Best practice: use a new address for every transaction. Modern HD wallets do this automatically.

🗝 The Key Derivation Chain

Your seed phrase (12 or 24 words) is the master key that generates ALL of your key pairs. One seed = billions of private keys, public keys, and addresses. This is why your seed phrase is so precious.
