Here's the trap: mandatory spending (Social Security, Medicare, interest) is locked in by law. It can't be cut without an act of Congress. Interest payments are contractual - miss one and the US defaults. That leaves almost no room to cut spending even if politicians wanted to.

So how does the government cover a $1.8 trillion annual deficit? The same way it always has: borrow more, and print the difference. The Federal Reserve buys Treasury bonds with money created from nothing, the government spends it, and your purchasing power silently erodes. This is the hidden tax you were never asked to vote on.
