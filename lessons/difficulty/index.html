Difficulty

No matter how many miners join or leave, a new block is always found approximately every 10 minutes. Bitcoin has a built-in difficulty adjustment that recalibrates every 2,016 blocks (~two weeks).

Think of it like a school exam that automatically adjusts. If too many students get answers too quickly, questions get harder. If students drop out, questions get easier. No one is in charge.

The difficulty is expressed as a target hash . The entire network's combined guessing speed is called the hash rate .

🎯 Self-Balancing Difficulty

Every 2,016 blocks, Bitcoin auto-adjusts to keep blocks at ~10 minutes. No human decides this.

The difficulty mechanism is one of Bitcoin's most elegant inventions. It means Bitcoin cannot be "sped up" by adding more computing power. The 10-minute block time is maintained through pure mathematics.
