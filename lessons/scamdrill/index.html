// The #1 way beginners lose bitcoin is not hacking. It is being asked nicely · ~8 min drill

Nobody breaks Bitcoin's cryptography. They break YOU - with urgency, authority, and greed. Billions of dollars are stolen every year through messages exactly like the ones below. Some of these are scams. Some are legitimate. Your job: tell them apart, before it costs you.

🔒 The five red flags, forever

1. Anyone asking for your seed phrase is a thief. No exceptions, no support teams, no "validations."

2. Urgency is a weapon. "Act in 10 minutes or lose your funds" means it is a scam - real systems do not work that way.

3. Guaranteed returns are guaranteed theft. "Send 1, get 2 back" has stolen more bitcoin than every hack combined.

4. Support never contacts you first. Unsolicited DMs, calls, and texts from "support" are scripts read by thieves.

5. Verify addresses character by character. Clipboard malware swaps addresses as you paste. Check the first six and last six, every time.

You can use Bitcoin safely now. Time to understand the machine - starting with the network no one can switch off.
