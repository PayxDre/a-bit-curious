Bitcoin Adoption

The adoption wave hiding in plain sight

Who actually uses Bitcoin: individuals, companies, ETFs, and nation states - the adoption picture in data. This is the reading version - the interactive lesson has the live demos, quizzes, and progress tracking.

One of the most persistent myths is that Bitcoin is just a speculative toy. In reality, the world's largest companies, financial institutions, and payment processors are racing to integrate it. Here's what's actually happening.

PAYMENTS Block (Square) Opens Bitcoin to Every Merchant

Jack Dorsey's Block has given every Square merchant the ability to accept Bitcoin payments. Millions of small businesses - coffee shops, restaurants, retail stores - can now receive Bitcoin directly. Cash App, also owned by Block, has enabled tens of millions of users to buy, sell, and send Bitcoin instantly. Block is also investing heavily in Bitcoin mining hardware and open-source Bitcoin development, making their commitment structural, not speculative.

LIGHTNING Lightning Network: Instant, Nearly Free Payments

The Lightning Network has turned Bitcoin into a real-time payment rail. Transactions settle in milliseconds for fractions of a penny. El Salvador's Chivo wallet runs on Lightning. Strike uses it for cross-border remittances that are faster and cheaper than Western Union. Businesses like Fold, Bitrefill, and thousands of merchants worldwide accept Lightning payments daily. What Visa does for the dollar, Lightning does for Bitcoin - but without the middlemen taking 2-3% of every transaction.

TREASURY Strategy (MicroStrategy): Bitcoin as a Treasury Reserve

Michael Saylor's Strategy (formerly MicroStrategy) has accumulated over 762,000 BTC[22], making it by far the largest corporate holder of Bitcoin in the world. At current prices, that's tens of billions of dollars in Bitcoin on a single corporate balance sheet. Why? Because holding cash means losing 7-10% of purchasing power per year to real inflation. Bitcoin offers a treasury asset with a fixed supply, no counterparty risk, 24/7 global liquidity, and a 17-year track record of outperforming every other asset class. The playbook is simple: instead of watching your cash reserves melt, convert them to the hardest money ever created. Strategy has continued to aggressively accumulate - issuing convertible notes, at-the-market equity offerings, and preferred stock specifically to acquire more Bitcoin. Since adopting this strategy, dozens of other public companies - including Tesla, Marathon Digital, Metaplanet, and Semler Scientific - have followed suit.

WALL STREET IBIT: The Most Successful ETP Launch in History

When the SEC approved spot Bitcoin ETFs in January 2024[21], BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT) shattered every record in ETP history. It reached $10 billion in assets faster than any ETF ever launched[21] - faster than gold ETFs, S&P 500 ETFs, bond ETFs, everything. Within months, the Bitcoin spot ETFs collectively held more Bitcoin than Satoshi Nakamoto's estimated stash. This wasn't retail speculation - this was pension funds, endowments, and sovereign wealth funds gaining exposure to Bitcoin through the world's most trusted financial infrastructure. Wall Street didn't kill Bitcoin. Wall Street adopted it.

FINANCIAL PRODUCTS Strike: Borrow Against Your Bitcoin, Never Sell

Strike has built lending products that let you borrow against your Bitcoin without ever selling it. Need cash for a house, a car, or an emergency? Put up your BTC as collateral, get a loan in dollars, and keep your upside exposure. When the loan is paid off, your Bitcoin comes back - and it may be worth far more than when you borrowed against it. This is the "never sell" strategy that the ultra-wealthy have used for generations with stocks and real estate - now available to anyone with Bitcoin.

FIXED INCOME Bitcoin-Backed Financial Products Enter Wall Street

Strategy (the company holding 762,000+ BTC) has started issuing traditional Wall Street instruments - preferred stock (STRC) and convertible notes - all backed by a corporate treasury denominated in Bitcoin. This is a big deal: for the first time, the $130+ trillion bond market has a bridge into Bitcoin. Pensions, endowments, and sovereign wealth funds that are required to hold fixed income instruments can now get Bitcoin exposure through products that fit their existing frameworks. Bitcoin is being embedded into the infrastructure of global capital markets.

Key terms

Lightning Network
A "Layer 2" protocol built on top of Bitcoin that enables instant, high-volume micropayments. Transactions settle in milliseconds for fractions of a penny.
"never sell" strategy
By borrowing against Bitcoin instead of selling, you avoid triggering capital gains taxes, keep your long-term position intact, and benefit from future appreciation. This is how the wealthy have always managed assets - borrow against, never sell.
preferred stock
Preferred stock sits between common stock and bonds. It pays a fixed dividend (like a bond) but represents ownership in the company (like a stock). It's a core instrument used by pensions, endowments, and insurance companies.
