Transactions

Build a transaction yourself: inputs, outputs, change, and the fee - the puzzle every wallet solves for you.

Open the interactive lesson →

A Bitcoin transaction is a digitally signed message that says "I, the owner of this Bitcoin, authorize sending this much to this address." It's like writing an unforgeable check that doesn't need a bank to process.

Every transaction has inputs and outputs . There's no "balance" stored anywhere - your balance is calculated by looking at all unspent outputs ever sent to your address.

Transactions include a transaction fee - like a tip to the miner. Bigger tip = faster service.

💸 Inputs In, Outputs Out

A transaction melts down coins you own and mints new ones for the people you are paying, plus change back to yourself. Anything left unclaimed goes to the miner as the fee.
