🎓 Your Next Steps

If this guide sparked your interest, here's the path forward, in order of priority:

Read the Bitcoin whitepaper

9 pages. You now have the background to understand every word. bitcoin.org/bitcoin.pdf

Download a wallet and buy a small amount

$10 is enough. Use a non-custodial wallet (Muun, Blue Wallet, or Phoenix). Send it to yourself. Feel how it works.

Read "The Bitcoin Standard" by Saifedean Ammous

The book that has converted more skeptics than any other. Covers the monetary history that makes Bitcoin's design choices click.

Run a node

A Raspberry Pi and a hard drive. You become part of the network that enforces the 21 million cap. This is how you stop trusting and start verifying.

Watch Saylor's "Bitcoin for Everybody"

Free MIT-level course. Goes deeper on the economics and game theory behind Bitcoin's design.
