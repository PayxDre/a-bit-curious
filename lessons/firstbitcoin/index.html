Knowledge that never becomes action is trivia. This lesson is the bridge: a step-by-step path to owning your first ten dollars of bitcoin with essentially zero risk, and graduating to real self-custody only when you have practiced every move with amounts that do not matter.

💡 The ten-dollar philosophy

Ten dollars buys you something no book can: real stakes, real buttons, real address anxiety, and real relief when it confirms. Every mistake you will ever make with Bitcoin, you want to make first with $10. Nobody has ever regretted practicing small.

Use a large, long-established, regulated exchange or payment app in your country. Boring and big beats new and exciting - you are optimizing for "still exists next year."

Watch it move. Feel the volatility on ten dollars instead of ten thousand. This is emotional training, and it is free.

Write the seed phrase on paper. Never photograph it, never type it into anything, never store it in a note app. You practiced key handling in the Lab - same rules, real stakes now.

This is the graduation exercise: your first real self-custody withdrawal. On-chain fees may eat a dollar or two of it - that lesson alone is worth the tuition. Many exchanges also support Lightning withdrawals, which cost pennies.

You now know the full loop: buy, hold, withdraw, custody. Whatever amount you choose from here, remember the rule from The Case Against Bitcoin: only what you can watch drop 70% without flinching.
